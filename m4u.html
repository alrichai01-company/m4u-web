About · Manufacturing

The house
behind the label.

Our Story

Makhija Trendz began with a simple conviction: Indian ethnic wear deserves manufacturing as refined as its design.

M4U is the result — a premium production house in Ahmedabad, rooted in one of India's great textile cities, where fabric selection, design, embroidery, stitching and quality control live under one roof, run to a standard our retail partners can build a business on.

The Journey

Growth, stitched in stages.

The Beginning

A workshop with a point of view

M4U starts as a focused kurti unit, supplying a handful of Ahmedabad boutiques who kept coming back.

Expansion

10,000+ sq. ft. of manufacturing

Demand from wholesalers takes the house into a full-scale facility — dedicated cutting, stitching and finishing floors.

The Collections Era

From garments to catalogs

Seasonal, retail-ready collections across kurtis, co-ords and suit sets — designed to sell through, not just sell in.

Today

Partners across India & beyond

Boutiques, distributors, online sellers and export buyers rely on M4U for consistency at scale.

The Process

Eight hands touch
every garment.

Fabric Selection

Mills are audited, swatches tested for shrinkage, colorfastness and hand-feel before approval.

Design Philosophy

Trend research meets retail data — every silhouette is designed to move off the rack.

Precision Cutting

Layered cutting with strict lay planning for consistent sizing across the full run.

Embroidery & Detail

In-house embellishment keeps detail work aligned to the original design intent.

Stitching

Skilled operators on modern machines, with inline checks at every station.

Quality Control

Three-stage inspection — cutting, stitching, finishing — before a piece is packed.

Finishing & Packing

Pressed, tagged and packed to arrive at your store retail-ready.

Privacy Policy.

M4U by Makhija Trendz ("we", "our") respects the privacy of every visitor and business partner. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit an inquiry, we collect the details you provide: name, mobile number, email, company name, location, business type and message.

How we use it

  • To respond to wholesale inquiries and share catalogs and pricing.
  • To manage orders, invoicing and dispatch.
  • To share new collection updates, where you have agreed to receive them.

What we don't do

We do not sell or rent your information. Details are shared only with logistics and payment partners as needed to fulfil your order.

Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information you share with us.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information at any time by contacting us through the details on our Contact page.
